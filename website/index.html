What we’re building

A practical assistant for the decisions between loads.

Weekly route planning ↗

Compare possible load sequences, deadhead, timing and where your truck will finish the week. Keep confirmed loads separate from forecasts.

Market context & weather ↗

Bring relevant market signals, seasonal context, weather and road restrictions into the decision. Data access and forecasting capabilities are being evaluated.

Documents & follow-ups ↗

Explore extracting dispatch details from rate confirmations and helping track revised documents, detention requests and next steps.

Help shape what comes next

Built around real dispatch work.

DDA is an independent project by Andrey Bytchenko, an experienced freight dispatcher. We’re exploring a small pilot with dispatchers and carriers, and conversations with data providers.

Early-stage prototype. No public product launch or live market forecast is offered on this site.
